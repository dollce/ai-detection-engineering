PHP2Shell 공격 탐지 E2E

참고: https://aws.amazon.com/ko/blogs/korea/using-aws-waf-to-apply-mitigations-for-php2shell-rce-vulnerabilities-cve-2026-109618-and-cve-2026-109619/
